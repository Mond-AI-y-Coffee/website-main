What are you building?

Building and launching the Holograph Company MVP

The core idea

End-of-life solutions are incomplete. Wills, insurance, and financial plans are easy to create, but only solve part of the problem. The gaps are: a place to store vital documents and communicate end-of-life plans, an automated solution for disposing of your digital accounts, a helper to reduce dependence on expensive lawyers.

What’s working / not working

  • Not (yet) working: Payments, document processing (such as death certificate), handover from principal to delegate at end of life, actual disposition of digital accounts
