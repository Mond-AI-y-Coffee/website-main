Mond(AI)y Coffee

What are you working on?

Running Mond(ai)y Coffee — a weekly Monday gathering in Atlanta for people curious about what AI is actually doing to the world. Also building out a home lab (Docker, Jellyfin, Tailscale, Home Assistant) and hunting for a better way to retain what I read and listen to.

AI interests

Practical AI over hype. I’m most interested in how AI changes the way small groups of people build things, and what it means for community and creative work. Less excited about enterprise decks, more excited about builders who show up on Mondays.

What are you looking for?

Good conversation and the occasional collaboration that starts over coffee and turns into something real.
