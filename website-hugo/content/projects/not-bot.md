Ken Griggs — Founder & CEO of Julia.social — will present **Not.Bot** at Mond(ai)y Coffee on Monday, July 27.

Not.Bot is privacy-protecting digital identity: cryptographic proof you're a real human, not a bot or AI, and signatures that verify who you are online. Ken will talk through what it takes to prove humanity in a world where anyone can spin up a convincing bot.

The full pre-read is still in progress. For now, come ready to hear how identity and trust hold up against generative AI, ask sharp questions, and help shape the conversation over coffee.
