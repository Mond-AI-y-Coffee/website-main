Ken Griggs — Founder & CEO of Julia.social — will present **Not.Bot** at Mond(ai)y Coffee on Monday, July 27.

## What are you building?

<a href="https://not.bot/" title="Visit not.bot" aria-label="Visit not.bot">not.bot</a> helps people distinguish humans from bots online now that AI can convincingly emulate people. Its first go-to-market use case is signing content so anyone can verify it: when publishers consistently sign their work, viewers can identify content falsely claiming to be from them. Future uses include AI-proof CAPTCHA and Sybil defense, document signing, and age verification.

## The core idea

<a href="https://not.bot/" title="Visit not.bot" aria-label="Visit not.bot">not.bot</a> is a maximally privacy-protecting form of digital identity. Privacy is Julia.social's corporate ethos: Julia, the character in Orwell's *1984*, best maintains her personal agency by protecting her privacy.

## What's working / not working

**Working:** The global app lets users scan signatures and enroll from U.S. app stores. A data-escrow partner captures user data; blockchain smart contracts are complete and open-source; the multiparty-computation engine, co-developed with Galois, is complete. Website integration components are released as **<a href="https://not.bot/" title="Visit not.bot" aria-label="Visit not.bot">not.bot</a> Verify**. The process-binding technology supporting honest.bot is implemented in <a href="https://not.bot/" title="Visit not.bot" aria-label="Visit not.bot">not.bot</a> Verify. The signing technology is patent-pending, and the <a href="https://not.bot/" title="Visit not.bot" aria-label="Visit not.bot">not.bot</a> and honest.bot trademarks are secured in the U.S. and multiple foreign jurisdictions.

**Not working yet:** The team is building **<a href="https://not.bot/" title="Visit not.bot" aria-label="Visit not.bot">not.bot</a> Sign My Work**, the initial GTM focus, targeting delivery by the end of August. Next come in-person enrollment and honest.bot.

## Spicy take

Between the KIDS Act (including KOSA, COPPA 2.0, and the SCREEN Act), the GUARD Act, Texas HB 1181, and Florida HB 3, age verification is coming to the U.S. internet. It has already arrived in places including the UK, France, Brazil, and Australia.
