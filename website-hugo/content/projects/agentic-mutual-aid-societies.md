James Harris - Entrepreneur-in-Residence and leader of ATDC's North Star AI Lab - presented **Agentic Mutual Aid Societies** at Mond(ai)y Coffee on Sunday, August 17.

James's vision for [buzz.xyz](https://buzz.xyz/) and the startup ecosystem is a modern-day coffeehouse: a place where startups share not just ideas, but tokens and GPUs.

What might mutual aid look like when agents can help a community coordinate its knowledge, computational resources, and upside?
