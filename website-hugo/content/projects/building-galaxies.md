Daniel Victorino — Founder & CEO of [Galaxies](https://www.galaxieslabs.com/) — will present **Building Galaxies** at Mond(ai)y Coffee on Monday, September 28.

Two halves: what Galaxies is, and how they're building it. Galaxies is an AI consumer-intelligence platform built on synthetic personas — digital twins of a brand's audience that teams can query like a continuous focus group. Daniel is visiting Atlanta from Brazil, where the company was founded and is now expanding into the U.S.
