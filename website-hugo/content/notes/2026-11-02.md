## Meeting Notes

Most of the job of ATDC is to help companies focus. That ain’t Mond(ai)y Coffee. It’s a room full of smart folks saying, “Don’t forget about…”

Chikamso Anayor Achu went through that gauntlet this morning. Chikamso shared AgenticReturn, a tool to help larger companies better track their token use. He left with 15 new product features and five more potential business lines.

Doing that every day would be too much, but opening your eyes to new possibilities every now and then is a good way to grow.
