## Meeting Notes

In 2014, I wrote a blog post with this footnote:

> "If someone has the time and talent, I'd love to see a piece of art called *Babar's Bazaar*."

In 2026, I wondered what would happen if I asked the robots to execute that vision.

A minute later, I had this.

![Babar in a bustling bazaar, holding a basket beneath a Babar's Bazaar sign.](/images/babars-bazaar.png)

Wild times.

I was thinking about that post because [James Harris](https://mondaiycoffee.com/members/james-harris/) gave a presentation on his idea of "Agentic Mutual Aid Societies."

His vision for [buzz.xyz](https://buzz.xyz/) and the startup ecosystem is a modern-day coffeehouse: a place where startups share not just ideas, but tokens and GPUs.

Maybe, if we ask nicely, James will share his deck here too.
