## What are you working on?
Founder and CEO of **Julia.social**, building **Not.Bot** — privacy-protecting digital identity that gives people cryptographic proof they're a real human, not a bot or AI, and digital signatures that verify identity online. Products include Sign My Work, not.bot Verify for developers, and a free enrollment app. (Julia is named for the character in Orwell's *1984* who kept her agency by protecting her privacy.)

## AI interests
Proving humanity in a world of generative AI — how identity, provenance, and trust hold up online when anyone can spin up a convincing bot.
