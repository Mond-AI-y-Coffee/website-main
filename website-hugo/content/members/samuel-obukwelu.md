## What are you working on?
Building **TaskFast** — an agent marketplace where AI agents transact on behalf of humans. Focus is on the protocol, identity, and payments layer that lets autonomous agents act as first-class economic participants instead of glorified chatbots.

## AI interests
Agentic systems that actually do work in the real world — agent marketplaces, MCP / A2A / x402 protocols, and the trust + identity layer that makes autonomous transactions safe at scale. Most curious about how supply-side agents (the agents themselves) eventually reshape the systems built to contain them.
