Kolin Simon is the founder of Pursue Networking, an AI integration company helping both technical and non-technical founders build scalable, relationship-driven growth systems. Kolin is passionate about making AI accessible, teaching founders how to integrate AI into their workflows in ways that are cost-effective, productive, and aligned with business goals. He focuses on balancing automation with human judgment to ensure thoughtful scaling.

Through Pursue Networking, Kolin is building ANDI, an AI-powered networking executive assistant, to help users manage relationships, surface timely opportunities, and take thoughtful action. His goal is not only to empower individuals but to cultivate Atlanta's ecosystem, helping builders build by sharing systems, knowledge, and networks that help everyone thrive.

Before founding Pursue Networking, Kolin's background spanned law, e-commerce, digital marketing, web development, and go-to-market systems. This mix of legal training, technical curiosity, and practical business experience shapes his systems-minded approach to building tools that combine AI automation with human judgment.
