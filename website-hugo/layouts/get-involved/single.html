Come on Monday

The easiest way to get involved is to just come on a Monday. No RSVP, no registration — just show up at 8:30am at ATDC and grab a cup.

Every meeting is a little different. Sometimes there's a structured presentation, sometimes it's an open conversation. Either way, you'll leave with new ideas and new contacts. First-timers are always welcome.

When Mondays, 8:30 – 10:00 am
Where ATDC · 75 5th St NW, Suite 2230, Atlanta, GA 30308

Keep the conversation going in Slack

Slack is where the community stays connected between Mondays. Email Adam for a Slack invite.

You don't need to join Slack or GitHub to attend. Just come for coffee.

Contribute a profile or pre-read

If you use GitHub, you can contribute profiles and pre-reads through the website repository. It's optional: emailing Adam works too. To find a talk worth sharing, start with the speaker guide.
